Mimi includes software and speech models made by others.

Speech models
- Parakeet TDT 0.6B V3, by NVIDIA. Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Converted to ONNX and quantized.
  https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3
- Whisper large-v3-turbo and small, by OpenAI. MIT License. Converted and quantized for whisper.cpp.
  https://github.com/openai/whisper
- Silero VAD, by Silero. MIT License.
  https://github.com/snakers4/silero-vad
